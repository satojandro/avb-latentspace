How to read the machine

A brain is a stack of layers. So is a model.

A neural network never stores its inputs. It turns them into activations, layer by layer, until everything it knows fits in a few numbers. That small space is called latent space, and it's where new things come from.

  1. 1 · Layers fireEach tray is part of my life.

    A lamp is one thing that shaped me. It lights when that part of me is in play.

  2. 2 · Signals fallEach layer feeds the next.

    A tray's bus bar sums its lamps and passes them down, the way each layer of a network transforms the one above.

  3. 3 · Squeeze26 activations become 2 numbers.

    The encoder compresses every lamp into z₁ and z₂. That pair is a point on the map: latent space.

  4. 4 · DecodeClose points, related ideas.

    The decoder turns the point into the nearest project. Drag across the map to explore the space between them.
