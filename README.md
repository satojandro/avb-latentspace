## What V1 was

A personal website told as an interactive isometric machine (built with the Anatomy skill's kit): a tiny
transformer whose MLP neurons are labelled as parts of the owner's life, with chapter pages for Caracas,
Melbourne, travel, crypto, AI, projects and contact. See `PLAN.md` for the V1 build notes.

## Goal carried into V2 (same project, same goal)

- A personal site that tells the owner's story: 18 years growing up in Venezuela (Caracas), Arts/Law in
  Melbourne, a lot of travel, several years in non-technical crypto roles, then building with AI.
- Immersive and interactive, not a traditional website: the story is told through an explainer of how
  latent space works in an actual LLM, with the model's neurons replaced by elements of the owner's life.
- A portfolio of the things the owner has built, coming out at the end.
- Content still needed from the owner: name, real influences per life chapter, real projects, travel list, links.
